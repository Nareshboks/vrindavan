Does the pillar score track performance?

The 8 pillars use the same tests as the single-ticker tracker: 5Yr P/E below 22.5, 5Yr ROIC above 9%, shares outstanding falling, and 5-year cash flow, net income and revenue growth positive, long-term liabilities under 5 times average free cash flow, and 5Yr price to free cash flow below 22.5. Fundamentals come from stockanalysis.com through your Firecrawl connector, 4 page reads (about 4 credits) per stock. Valuation uses the price in your CSV. ETFs, warrants, cash and some foreign listings have no pillar data. Positions and pillar results stay in this browser. Not investment advice.
